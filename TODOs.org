**** prompt
You are working in a project containing a source PDF and possibly a partially converted Emacs Org-mode document with extracted image assets. Your task is to complete or improve the conversion of the PDF into a readable, navigable, accurate Org document.

## Goal
Produce a faithful Org-mode version of the book, preserving its structure, prose, mathematics, figures, captions, and end matter. Treat the PDF as the source of truth. Keep the PDF unchanged.

## First inspect the project
- Inspect the existing Org file, asset directory, and any project conversion plan before editing.
- Follow the existing document structure, naming conventions, math notation, image-link style, and plan workflow.
- Identify what is already converted and what remains; do not overwrite good existing work or duplicate content.
- If a plan exists, follow it in order and keep its status current as required by the project instructions.

## Conversion requirements
- Preserve the book’s front matter, chapter and subsection hierarchy, navigation/contents, prose, equations, symbols, subscripts, superscripts, annotations, citations, captions, and end matter.
- Use Org headings and LaTeX-compatible math consistent with the existing document. Check ambiguous or math-heavy material against the rendered PDF, not just text extraction.
- Keep useful source-page references where they aid traceability.
- Do not silently summarize or omit source content. If exact transcription is impractical, clearly identify the limitation rather than presenting a summary as a complete conversion.

## Figures and adjacent text
- Each linked figure asset must contain the figure itself and, where appropriate, its caption—not unrelated surrounding paragraphs, headers, or page text.
- Transcribe meaningful prose adjacent to or below a figure into the correct place in the Org document, preserving its context and reading order. Do not lose text when cropping an image.
- Crop carefully from the PDF-rendered page. Inspect the crop to ensure the complete diagram, labels, legend, and caption are retained and neighboring prose is excluded.
- Render vector figures at sufficiently high resolution for comfortable reading (normally 300 DPI or higher when appropriate). Do not merely upscale low-resolution images; check the source’s native quality for raster figures.
- Keep existing relative asset paths and Org links when suitable. Verify every link resolves to a present asset.
- Leave bibliography and index page images unchanged if they are intentionally retained as scans; do not transcribe or recrop them unless explicitly requested.

## Validation
- Compare the resulting document against the PDF across the full page range, with extra attention to equations, figure-dependent explanations, and extraction artifacts.
- Check that headings and contents match, paragraphs are in the correct order, and there is no duplicated or missing text.
- Inspect every figure asset being changed; verify figure/caption pairing, clean boundaries, and readable resolution.
- Check that all Org image links resolve, math environments are balanced, and the PDF remains unchanged.
- Report what was changed, what was checked, and any remaining limitations. Do not claim complete validation if only a sample was reviewed.

Work incrementally and preserve existing correct content. Before editing, briefly state the next concrete scope of work; after validation, report the files changed and evidence gathered.
